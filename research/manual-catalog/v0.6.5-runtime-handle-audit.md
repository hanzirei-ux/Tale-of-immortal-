## Limitations and integrity
- This confirms the **static call-site/token/default data relationship** but does **not** establish that Unity instantiated the runtime FieldInfo object with those values during execution.
- The semantic relationship between the prefix number stored in save instances and `ConfBattleSkillPrefixValueItem.number` remains **unproven**. Consequently, the 317/515 numeric matches are conditional rather than ready-to-use manual definitions.
- Final display **names and levels for 183 manual instances** remain unverified. Add Manual is **not implemented**.
- Preserve Mod Menu v0.3.6 DEX and all working gameplay features. Do not fabricate attributes or patch native methods prematurely.
