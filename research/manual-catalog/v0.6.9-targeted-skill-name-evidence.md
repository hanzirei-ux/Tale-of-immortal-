## Crucial limitation discovered in inventory data

Of **183** observed manual instances in the sample save, **80** have the same generic Ability BaseID **`81001`** across different grades. This strongly limits what a **BaseID-to-name** lookup can accomplish: those 80 objects cannot safely be given a single specific manual title. We must reconstruct names from *per-instance* values/prefix and the game's `SkillInfoData.GetName` mechanism or other verified runtime data.

After the new targeted candidates, the research evidence tiers for the 183 observed instances are:
- 49 matched by previously direct-linked base names;
- 2 supported only by previously weak stem/attack-term association;
- 1 contextual new Rainbow Palm association;
- 5 covered by new **unverified** wiki mechanics candidates;
- 126 without even a base-name candidate (including the 80 instances using `81001`).

**None of the 183 final book display names or Levels have been independently verified.** Their known Grade, Martial Type and BaseID are separate attributes and must remain unchanged.
