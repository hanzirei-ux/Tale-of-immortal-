## Audit of the supplied resources

We directly inspected `file 3.zip` in addition to the binary/metadata traces:

- The archive has **213 actual non-directory files**.
- Its `PackManifest.json` references **53,987 asset-bundle names**, but **zero `.ab` files are present inside that particular archive**. A manifest references assets; it does not contain their values.
- No file actually named `LocalText.json` or `BattleSkillPrefixName.json` was supplied in the archive.
- 30 sharedasset/asset-split segments were scanned for literal UTF-8 representations of the 71 name keys; **zero plain-string hits**.
- `global-metadata.dat` includes IL2CPP string literals such as `skill_name_middle14111` and English skill descriptions, but these **are not an explicit dictionary mapping every localization key to its English value**.

**Important limitation:** A zero hit from these scans **does not prove the translations are absent from the game or even from compressed/specially serialized Unity TextAssets**. Most asset bundles referenced by the manifest are **not present in the provided archive**.

The audit recovered **0 additional build-verified English translations**. The previous 49 manual instances with strong English base-name evidence remain **English title candidates**, not UI-confirmed final manual names; static reconstructed Levels remain unconfirmed by live UI.
