## Native prefix-name tables recovered

The original matching `libil2cpp.so` and `global-metadata.dat` contain **400 calls** to the `ConfBattleSkillPrefixNameItem` constructor at RVA `0x2827be4`. Constructor arguments were read directly from ARM64: row ID in `w1`; name-group ID in `w2`; Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Korean string pointers in `x3`, `x4`, `x5` and `x6`. Precisely **200 ordered entries in group 1 (IDs 1–200)** and **200 in group 2 (IDs 201–400)** were decoded. **400/400 entries have all four language strings**, no empty English fields or Han-character-bearing English fields. The decoder handles an ARM64 `LSR X4,X3,#0` pointer alias found in one row.

Native `ConfBattleSkillPrefixName.GetName` at `0x52c0d28` selects group 1 and group 2 and indexes the ordered lists with the previously v0.9.9-reconstructed `checksum3*123 % 200` and `checksum5*456 % 200` selectors. If two selected strings compare equal, the code reselects the second with `((checksum5*456)|1) % 200`. **The 183 sampled English pairs do not collide**, so no reroute applies to those cases. Locale-aware joiner `0x456d800` inserts a single space between English prefix parts, and `SkillInfoData.GetName` at `0x5840ed4` inserts another single space before the base English name. The two 200-entry lists are distinct from the separate 63-key `skill_name_first` and `skill_name_second` language entries.
