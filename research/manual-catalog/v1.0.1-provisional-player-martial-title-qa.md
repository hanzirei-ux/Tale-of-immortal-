## QA package and regression

The private chat-delivered `TOI_v101_Player_Martial_Title_QA.zip` contains a 26-row per-instance JSONL/CSV, 6-example `ANDROID_CHECKLIST.csv`, reproducible read-only title/Level reconstruction and a comparison tool `compare_android_v101.py` accepting **actual user-entered** game observations. The comparator differentiates `MATCH`, `MISMATCH`, `NOT_VISIBLE`, `NOT_CHECKED` without promoting unobserved names to verified. A sample match cannot establish the correctness of unrelated instances.
