# Tale of Immortal Android — v1.1.9 AssetPack and Runtime Catalog Audit

**2026-11-03 | Read-only audit of user-provided game files.** No APK, DEX, native library, save, or gameplay action was modified.

## Inputs and inventory

- Seventeen sequential `assetpack_001.zip`–`assetpack_017.zip` archives were inspected without bulk-extracting private game assets.
- **53,474** unique `.ab` UnityFS bundle filenames were present, plus one non-`.ab` `AssetBundle` record (**53,475** archive entries total).
- The accompanying private `PackManifest.json` listed **53,987** bundles. Exactly **512** manifest-listed bundles were absent from these archives: **467** `texture_game_portraitdynamic_woman_zuiba_*` and **45** `texture_game_portraitdynamic_woman_zuolian_*`. These relate to portrait mouth/face animation textures and are not the selected item/skill sprite atlases.
- Private `file 3.zip` also contains save/cache files; those were not analyzed or changed.

## Genuine Android JNI4 collector: 3,284 visible Item IDs

The original v0.3.6 JSONL contains **3,284** `kind=item` records with **3,284 unique Item IDs** and unambiguous runtime-provided ID, display-name, Type and native Level fields. Counts by `ConfItemProps.type`:

| Raw type | Category | Visible |

These counts reproduce the earlier native-research total of 3,309 configured items: **25 items were hidden** and therefore not present in the JNI4 export.

### Gear cross-check

The prior verified native subtype totals still match the runtime Gear IDs by category-oriented ID/name group audit:

- **Ring:** 34.
- **Mount:** 56.
- **Outfit:** 295.
- **Artifact:** 10.
- **Total Gear:** 395.

**393/395** Gear have runtime displayed localized names. **2 Outfit** records expose only `item_name...` placeholders. Across the complete 3,284-row collector, **60** display-name fields are fallback `item_name<ID>` strings. An ID/name-group assignment is not a substitute for a native per-row subtype field; the JNI4 JSONL has `subtype=null`.

### UnityFS atlas inspection

Ten selected UnityFS sprite atlases in the private AssetPack were decompressed successfully and yielded **2,447 distinct internal candidate sprite-path strings**. Notably:

- `spriteatlas_commonpropiconspriteatlas.ab`: **1,210** candidate item/property sprite paths.
- `spriteatlas_commonskilliconspriteatlas.ab`: **665** candidate skill sprite paths.

These are extracted serialized resource **path strings**, **not PNG exports and not exact ItemID-to-icon or SkillID-to-icon joins**. Count must not be interpreted as 1,210 collectible item icons or 665 skills.

## Known limitations

- The runtime `ConfItemProps.level` is **not equivalent to verified Grade, Realm, or Rarity**.
- The collector does not provide per-row native subtype, translated English name for 60 fallback keys, icon join, per-item Grade/Realm/Rarity, or native addability proof.
- The **860** constructor-backed named Manual/Skill templates and **810** name components remain separately reported by earlier native research. Neither is an ordinary inventory ItemID, and neither is contained as full entries in this JNI4 item JSONL.
- The **11** raw Type 2 Books are **not** interchangeable with the 860 Skill/Manual constructor templates.
- No new in-game Add Item, Add Manual, DEX integration, or Android behavior is claimed. The private local HTML/CSV/XLSX catalogs are read-only QA aids.

## Privacy

**Only this aggregate report** is committed to public GitHub. Full game archives, binaries, private saves, per-item extracted lists, atlas row datasets and the user-facing private catalog ZIP are **not uploaded**. Preserve the working v0.3.6 Mod Menu and existing native/JNI pathways.
